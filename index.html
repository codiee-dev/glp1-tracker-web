Your GLP-1 week, in one calm place.

Weekwell reminds you on shot day, logs your dose in one tap, rotates your injection sites and keeps side effects, weight, protein and water together. No account, and your journal stays on your iPhone.

Get it on the App Store

What's inside

  • Any medication and any dose: pens, pens dosed in clicks, vials and daily pills, weekly or every few days
  • Discreet reminders you can mark as taken right from the notification
  • A body map that suggests the next injection site
  • Side effects, food noise, water and protein in a few taps, or just say it
  • Weight by dose, with Apple Health, and an estimated medication level
  • Gentle streaks that never break for a pause or a rough day
  • Widgets, a shot-day Live Activity, and a PDF report for your doctor
  • In English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Turkish

Logging, your full history and export are free forever. Weekwell Pro adds forecasts, insights, the doctor report and more.
